## 📌 Feature Details

### **🎯 Aimbot**
- **FOV & Smoothness** – Adjustable aiming field and transition speed.
- **Visibility Check** – Only locks onto visible targets.
- **Target Priority** – Choose by FOV, distance, or health.
- **Recoil Compensation** – Reduces weapon kick.
- **Aim Method** – Memory or Mouse-based aiming.

### **👁️ Player ESP**
- **Boxes** – Normal, Corner, or Filled.
- **Skeleton & Health** – Visualizes enemy posture and status.
- **Snaplines & Offscreen Arrows** – Tracks distant players.
- **Customizable Font & Distance** – Adjust display to your preference.

### **🤖 Drones & Loot ESP**
- **Multi-Drone Tracking** – Supports **20+ drone types** (Wasp, Hornet, Queen, etc.).
- **Loot Filtering** – Highlights valuable items by rarity.
- **Container Categories** – Lockers, Crates, Vehicles, Electronics, and more.

### **⚙️ Additional Features**
- **Battle Mode** – Optimized for intense combat.
- **Config System** – Save and load multiple setups.
- **Weapon Mods** – No recoil, rapid fire, and flashlight customization.
 **Works in both Windowed & Borderless modes**
